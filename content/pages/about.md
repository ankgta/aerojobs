{{ site.site_name }} brings open jobs from airlines, airports, maintenance companies and aerospace manufacturers around the world into one searchable board.

## Why it exists

Aviation careers are international. A first officer in India might be looking at airlines in the Gulf. A licensed engineer in Europe might be weighing a move to Asia. Yet every employer publishes its vacancies on its own careers site, each laid out differently. Checking dozens of them by hand takes hours, and job boards that charge employers to post miss many of the roles that matter.

This site does the checking for you. Twice a day it reads the public careers pages of the employers we cover and lists every open role with the same filters: country, job type, work mode and how recently the job was posted.

## How the listings work

- **Straight from the employer.** Jobs come from each employer's own careers site. We do not accept paid placements in the main listings.
- **You apply with the employer.** Every listing links to the original job page. We never handle applications or ask for your CV.
- **Kept current.** When an employer removes a job, it comes off this board within a day.
- **Short summaries.** Each listing has a brief summary written by us. The full job description is on the employer's site.

Some careers pages do not give a posting date. For those jobs we show the date we first found them, marked "First seen".

## What we never do

We never charge job seekers, and no genuine airline or airport does either. If anyone asks you to pay for an interview, training or a visa to get a job, read our guide before you go any further: [how to spot a fake airline job offer]({{ link('guides/spot-fake-airline-job-offers') }}).
