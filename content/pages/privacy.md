This policy explains what information {{ site.site_name }} ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- You can search and read every job without an account and without giving us any personal details.
- We use cookies for visitor statistics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense).
- Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before these cookies are used.
- When you click "Apply", you leave this site. The employer's own privacy policy then applies.

## Information we collect

**Information you send us.** If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply. We do not use it for marketing.

**Usage information.** Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. With your consent where required, Google Analytics also collects information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and approximate location, to help us improve it.

We do not collect CVs, job applications or account details.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use:

- **Analytics cookies** (Google Analytics) to count visits and understand which pages are useful.
- **Advertising cookies** (Google AdSense) to show ads and measure them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

## Advertising

We show ads served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/). Learn more in [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message, provided through Google's certified consent tool, and can accept or refuse these cookies and change their choice later.

## Links to employers

Job listings link to employers' careers sites. We do not control those sites, and their own privacy policies apply when you apply for a job.

## How long we keep information

Emails are kept for up to two years, then deleted. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in Google Analytics (14 months).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us at [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) and we will respond within one month.

## Children

This site is meant for adults looking for work. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
