How it works

Everything runs in GitHub at 05:23 and 17:23 UTC. Nothing runs on your computer.

The fetcher obeys every site's robots.txt, keeps facts only (never full job descriptions), and hides jobs that disappear two runs in a row.

Where things live

Only the Claude side belongs to a Claude account. Everything else stays put if you move accounts.
